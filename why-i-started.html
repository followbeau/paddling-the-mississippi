Why I Decided to Canoe the Mississippi River

Starting at the headwaters meant choosing uncertainty and committing to the journey one day at a time.

The decision to go

I wanted to experience the Mississippi River at a pace that would let me see more than a map or a highway could show me. Traveling by canoe meant paying attention to the water, the weather, and the small towns along the way.

Beginning at Lake Itasca

I began my solo trip in July 2026 at the river’s source in Minnesota. The early miles were slower than I expected, and moving a loaded canoe around obstacles reminded me that even an ambitious plan has to adapt to what is happening in front of you.

Why the challenge mattered

The goal was not just to cover miles. I wanted to test my patience and independence while documenting an experience that felt personal to me. Each day on the river gave me a new reason to keep going.
