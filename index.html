About Me and This Journey

I'm a student and outdoor adventurer who set out to paddle the Mississippi River alone in July 2026. I chose this topic because the trip challenged me in ways that are difficult to explain with just a mileage total. From the headwaters in Minnesota to long days navigating wind, gear, and unfamiliar water, I had plenty to write about.

This website shares my own perspective on why I started, what made the trip difficult, and what I learned along the way. I wanted to create a place where friends, classmates, and anyone interested in canoe travel could follow the experience.
