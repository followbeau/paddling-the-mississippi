What Being Alone on the River Taught Me

A solo canoe trip can change how you think about patience, self-reliance, and progress.

Progress is not always fast

Early in the trip, I learned that a day with fewer miles could still be productive. Weather, portages, and the need to rest were part of the experience rather than interruptions to it.

Paying attention

Traveling slowly made it easier to notice the character of each section of river. I began to appreciate quiet mornings, changing scenery, and the small routines that kept me moving.

What I took away

The trip challenged me physically, but it also reinforced the value of patience. I learned to make practical decisions, accept uncertainty, and appreciate the experience instead of measuring every day only by distance.
